      2019-2020 Season Robot

DRAGONFLY

Robot Overview

  • ●Extremely powerful X-shaped linkage high-lift arm
  • ●Main gripper with crank-slider and elastic linkage, belt-driven side gripper arm
  • ●Uses "stack on stack" method, mechanism can reach maximum height of 15 blocks
  • ●Extremely robust autonomous control and algorithms: OpenCV vision recognition + composite correction system

🏅 Season Achievements

  • 🔧 Nanjing Invitational - Design Award - December 2019
  • 🥇 Beijing Student Robot Competition FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) - First Prize - December 2019
  • ⭐ China Championship Online Award Selection - Inspire Award - August 2020
