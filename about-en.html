❤️ About Us

Team Introduction

"Beyond the Bots" is our core creed, driving us from a competition team to a STEM innovation community beyond the arena. We pursue technical excellence while believing in the power of sharing and mutual assistance, and take it as our mission to actively promote the common growth of the entire technical ecosystem.

Team Number: 19705

Established: 2017

Location: Beijing, China

Our Mission

To help every member grow through the process of creating robots, and help more partners grow together. To this end, we insist on technical sharing, are willing to lend a helping hand, and actively explore how to use our projects to serve the campus and society, so that our passion can resonate beyond the arena.
