Team Introduction

Who We Are

FTC Team 19705 WXYZ was established in 2018. We are a passionate and innovative student robotics team from Beijing, China. Over the past 8 seasons, we have continuously challenged ourselves on the FTC stage, achieving remarkable results.

Our Vision

We believe robotics competition is not just about winning, but a journey of learning, growth, and making friends. We are committed to spreading FIRST values in the community, helping more students discover the joy of STEM, and growing together with teams worldwide.

Core Values

Gracious Professionalism: Compete fiercely while respecting every opponent
Coopertition: Achieve stronger competition through stronger cooperation
Continuous Learning: Learn from failure and grow from success
Team Spirit: Every member is indispensable

🌟 Highlights

From rookie team to championship contenders, from learning basics to mastering advanced technologies, from competing alone to helping other teams - every step is an unforgettable memory. We are proud of our growth and grateful to everyone who has supported us.

What We Do

🤖 Robot Design & Build

Design and build competitive robots from scratch using CAD modeling, mechanical structures, electronic circuits, and programming. Each season's robot embodies our team's innovation and technical skills.

💻 Programming & Algorithms

Develop autonomous navigation, computer vision recognition, sensor fusion and other advanced algorithms. We continuously optimize code to make robots more intelligent and reliable.

📝 Engineering Documentation

Maintain detailed engineering notebooks recording design processes, test data, problem-solving methods, etc. This not only helps us review and improve, but also shares experiences with other teams.

🌍 Outreach Activities

Participate in STEM education promotion, share robotics knowledge with more students, and help rookie teams grow. We believe knowledge sharing makes the entire community stronger.

🏅 Competition Participation

Participate in regional qualifiers, championships, and international competitions. Each competition is an opportunity to test our skills, learn from excellent teams, and make new friends.

🤝 Teamwork

Learn project management, task allocation, communication and collaboration through teamwork. These skills will benefit us throughout our lives.
